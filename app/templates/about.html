About this app

The Mentalist Explorer covers all 151 episodes of the acclaimed CBS crime drama across 7 seasons, with detailed storylines, US viewership trends, IMDb ratings, character allegiances, and award history. It runs natively as a Databricks App querying Delta tables in Unity Catalog.

How it's built

Built on Databricks
Databricks Apps · Unity Catalog · Delta Lake · Serverless SQL Warehouse

Data was loaded once from curated datasets into Unity Catalog. The app reads and caches it into memory when it boots.
